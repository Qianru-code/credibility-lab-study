Credibility Lab — Reading Group Participant Guide

Your session details

The researcher will provide these before you begin:

  • Participant code: __________
  • Assigned group: Reading group
  • Pre-test form: A / B (researcher to specify)
  • Post-test form: the other form
  • Researcher contact and file-return channel: __________

Ask the researcher if any detail is missing. Use the same assigned code throughout. Do not include your name, email address or other identifying information in your answers or filenames. A and B are form names, not fixed pre-test or post-test labels.

What you will do

Pre-test → Assigned reading → Post-test → Return your files and receive the debrief

This study explores how university students evaluate claims in social-media posts. In the reading group, you read two prepared examples and their source notes between assessments. You do not need to use the social-feed training or complete the website experience questionnaire.

Read the participant information supplied by the researcher and complete the consent process before starting. Participation is voluntary: you may skip items, ask for help or stop. See the participant information sheet for contact, withdrawal and data-use arrangements. This guide does not replace that information or consent process.

The existing study guide suggests allowing approximately 40–50 minutes; this is a planning estimate, not a time limit. Use a computer and browser that can download JSON files. Work individually and open each activity only when you reach its step. Tell the researcher before beginning if you need translation or language assistance, and report any assistance used. If you have already completed the website training or these assessment forms, tell the researcher before starting.

1. Complete the pre-test

Open only the form assigned for your pre-test:

  • Form A
  • Form B

Enter your assigned Participant code and select Pre-test under Stage. Check both fields before answering.

Each form contains six fictional campus claims. For each claim:

  • Select Supported, Misleading or Insufficient evidence.
  • Rate your confidence from 0 (no confidence) to 100 (completely confident).
  • Briefly explain the evidence or reasoning behind your judgement, usually in one or two sentences. You may leave an item unanswered if you prefer.

Supported means the available information supports the claim as written. Misleading means the claim gives a misleading impression of the evidence. Insufficient evidence means there is too little information to decide.

You may consult either, both or neither of the two source notes supplied with each assessment item. Do not open the reading activity or the other assessment form yet. Ask for procedural help if needed; do not seek correctness feedback while completing an assessment.

Select Submit responses, then Download a backup. Confirm that the JSON file is saved before closing the page. If your version instead has Download responses, use that download button. Keep this original pre-test file; do not overwrite it later.

2. Read the assigned materials

After saving your pre-test, open the reading activity.

Read both the Great Wall and Venus examples, their source notes and the explanations headed What the evidence shows. Follow the instructions on the reading page. Complete the reading before opening the post-test.

For this assigned activity, use the reading sheet rather than the interactive social feed. Tell the researcher when you finish and report any help or technical problem. Record the reading start and finish times if requested. You do not need to create a profile or download a website training record.

3. Complete the post-test

Open the other form:

  • If your pre-test was A, your post-test is B.
  • If your pre-test was B, your post-test is A.

Enter the same participant code and select Post-test under Stage. Follow the same response instructions and source-note options as in the pre-test. Answer independently; there is no requirement to improve or change your responses.

Submit and download the JSON backup. Check that it is a separate file from your pre-test. Do not complete both forms at each stage or repeat an assessment to replace an answer. If you accidentally open or complete an extra form, tell the researcher and retain the files so the actual sequence can be recorded.

4. Return your files

Send two JSON attachments through the agreed channel:

  • Your pre-test, for example P12-Pre-test-A.json.
  • Your post-test, for example P12-Post-test-B.json.

These filenames are examples; use your assigned code and actual form order. Return both files even if an online receipt appears. Automatic delivery may fail. If you see Receipt unavailable, keep the page open, download the backup and check your Downloads folder. Tell the researcher if a file cannot be saved. Do not edit the answers or stage inside a JSON file; report any mistake instead.

Tell the researcher when you are finished and request the debrief or clarification about the claims. Reading-group participants do not complete the website experience questionnaire because they have not used the website training.
