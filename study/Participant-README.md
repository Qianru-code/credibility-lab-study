# Credibility Lab participant guide · 参与者指南

Credibility Lab is a simulated social network for a university research project. We are studying how students judge claims in social-media posts and what it is like to use the site. The accounts are fictional. Posts and reactions you create in the feed stay in your browser. The study forms and website buttons are in English.

Credibility Lab 是一个大学研究项目使用的模拟社交网络。我们希望了解大学生怎样判断社交媒体帖子中的说法，以及使用这个网站的体验。网站里的账号是虚构的，你在信息流中发帖或做出的互动保存在自己的浏览器里。网站和表单使用英文，本指南保留了按钮原名，方便查找。

## Before you start · 开始前

Read the participant information supplied by your researcher and complete the consent process if you wish to take part. Participation is voluntary. You may skip assessment or questionnaire items, ask for help or stop at any time. Allow approximately 40–50 minutes for the session. A computer with a recent browser is easiest for writing and saving files.

请先阅读研究人员提供的参与者说明。如果愿意参加，请先完成知情同意。你可以跳过测评或问卷中的题目、询问操作方法，也可以随时停止。整场预计约 40–50 分钟。建议使用电脑和较新的浏览器，方便输入理由和保存文件。

Have your **participant code**, **assigned group** and **first form letter (A or B)** ready. If any is missing, ask the researcher who invited you. Use the same code throughout, and leave your name and contact details out of your answers. Work individually. Open each activity when you reach its step.

请确认你已收到**参与者编号、组别和第一份试卷的字母（A 或 B）**。缺少任何一项，请联系邀请你的研究人员。全程使用同一编号，答案中请勿填写姓名或联系方式。请独立作答，按下面的顺序打开材料。

| Your group · 你的组别 | Your route · 操作顺序 |
|---|---|
| Interactive · 网站互动组 | Pre-test → Social feed → Post-test → Website experience questionnaire / 前测 → 网站练习 → 后测 → 网站体验问卷 |
| Reading · 阅读组 | Pre-test → Assigned reading → Post-test / 前测 → 指定阅读材料 → 后测 |

## 1. Complete your pre-test · 两组先做前测

Open your assigned form, enter your code under **Participant code**, and choose **Pre-test** under **Stage**. Each form contains six fictional campus posts. For each post, choose a judgement, enter your confidence from 0 (no confidence) to 100 (completely confident), and briefly explain your reason. You may open either, both or neither of the two source notes.

打开分配给你的试卷，在 **Participant code** 填编号，在 **Stage** 选 **Pre-test（前测）**。每份卷有六条虚构校园帖子。每题选一个判断，填写你对该判断的把握程度（0＝完全没把握，100＝非常有把握），再简短说明理由。每题有两份来源材料，可以看一份、两份或都不看。

| Option · 选项 | Meaning · 含义 |
|---|---|
| Supported · 有证据支持 | The available information supports the claim as written. / 现有信息支持帖子实际提出的说法。 |
| Misleading · 具有误导性 | The claim gives a misleading impression of what the information shows. / 帖子的说法让人对现有信息产生误解。 |
| Insufficient evidence · 证据不足 | The available information is too limited to decide. / 现有信息不足以作出判断。 |

Click **Submit responses**. Once you see **Responses received** and a receipt number, continue to your assigned learning activity. / 点击 **Submit responses（提交答案）**。看到 **Responses received（已收到）** 和回执编号后，开始分配给你的学习活动。

[Open Form A · 打开 A 卷](https://qianru-code.github.io/credibility-lab-study/study/Form-A.html) | [Open Form B · 打开 B 卷](https://qianru-code.github.io/credibility-lab-study/study/Form-B.html)

## 2. Complete your assigned activity · 完成本组学习任务

**Interactive group · 网站互动组**

1. Open the social feed in a fresh browser session. Go to **My profile → Edit profile**, set **Display name** to your participant code, then select **Save profile**. Use the same browser session until you have saved your activity record. / 打开一个新的浏览器会话进入信息流，在 **My profile → Edit profile** 把 **Display name** 改为参与者编号，再点 **Save profile**。请在同一个浏览器会话中完成练习并保存活动记录。
2. Browse **Home**, visit an author’s profile, bookmark a post, and find it again in **Bookmarks**. / 浏览首页，打开一位作者的主页，收藏一条帖子，再到 **Bookmarks（收藏）** 找回它。
3. Find the **Great Wall** post, then the **Venus** post. For each, choose **Take a closer look**, enter your initial judgement and confidence, and explore the checking materials. Submit your final judgement, selected evidence and explanation, then read the feedback. You may keep or change your initial judgement. If you want to skip a required training step, tell the researcher. / 先找**长城**帖，再找**金星**帖。每条都点 **Take a closer look**，作初判、填信心值、查看核查材料，再提交终判、所选证据及理由，阅读反馈。你可以保留或改变初判。如果想跳过某个必填训练步骤，请告诉研究人员。
4. Return to the feed and reopen one completed judgement. Go to **Activity → Download activity and training record**. Save the file using your code, for example `P01-training.json`, and return it through the channel your researcher provided. The researcher receives your training record when you return this file. / 返回信息流，重新打开一次已完成的判断。进入 **Activity**，点 **Download activity and training record**。用自己的编号保存文件，例如 `P01-training.json`，通过研究人员指定的渠道交回。研究人员通过这份文件收到你的网站练习记录。

[Open the social feed · 进入网站练习](https://qianru-code.github.io/credibility-lab-study/#home)

**Reading group · 阅读组**

Open the reading sheet supplied by your researcher. Read both examples and their source notes. When you finish, proceed to the post-test. / 打开研究人员发给你的阅读材料，读完两个案例及相应的来源说明，然后进入后测。

## 3. Complete your post-test · 两组都做后测

Use the other form. If you started with A, now open B. If you started with B, now open A. Enter the same participant code and select **Post-test**. Judge the six new posts using the same instructions as before. Submit and wait for your receipt. / 换另一份试卷：前测做 A，后测就做 B；前测做 B，后测就做 A。填同一编号，在 **Stage** 选 **Post-test（后测）**。按前测相同的方法判断六条新帖子，提交并等待回执。

[Open the study forms · 打开试卷入口](https://qianru-code.github.io/credibility-lab-study/study/)

## 4. Tell us about the website · 网站互动组填写体验问卷

After the post-test, website users complete the experience questionnaire with the same code. For the rating statements, the circles run from **Strongly agree** on the left to **Strongly disagree** on the right. Choose **N/A** for a feature you did not use or cannot judge. Select the task-completion option that describes what happened, including any help you received. Submit and wait for your receipt. / 后测结束后，用过网站的同学请用同一编号填写体验问卷。评分题从左至右是**完全同意到完全不同意**；未使用某功能或无法判断时选 **N/A**。任务完成情况请按实际体验填写，包括是否获得过帮助。提交后等待回执。

[Open the website experience questionnaire · 打开网站体验问卷](https://qianru-code.github.io/credibility-lab-study/study/questionnaire.html)

## If a receipt does not appear · 提交后没有回执怎么办

If you see **Receipt unavailable**, keep the page open. Click **Download a backup** and check that the `.json` file is in your Downloads folder. Send it to the researcher through the agreed channel and say which form you completed. You can also retry while the page is open. Once you have a receipt or a saved backup, you can close the page. / 如果看到 **Receipt unavailable（未能取得回执）**，请保持页面打开。点 **Download a backup（下载备份）**，在下载目录确认有 `.json` 文件，再通过约定渠道交给研究人员，注明完成了哪份表。也可以在当前页面重试。确认已拿到回执或保存好备份后再关页面。

At the end, contact the researcher who invited you for the debrief or for clarification about any claim. Please use the contact and withdrawal arrangements in your participant information sheet. Thank you for taking part. / 完成后，请联系邀请你的研究人员获取结束说明，也可以询问对哪些帖子还有疑问。联系方式及退出安排见参与者说明书。谢谢你的参与。
